# Privacy Policy for Unbound

**Last Updated:** May 2026

Welcome to Unbound. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you play our game. Unbound is designed to be playable without an account, and we prioritize keeping your game progress and settings local to your device.

## 1. What Data We Collect
**On-Device Data:** Game progress, sound preferences, and puzzle activity are stored locally on your device. This specific gameplay data does not leave your phone.

**Automatically Collected Data:** To serve advertisements and monitor game health (e.g., crash reporting), certain technical data may be transmitted to our partners (like Google AdMob). This can include:
- Device identifiers (such as Advertising IDs).
- Usage data (how you interact with the game and ads).
- Approximate location (at the regional or city level, to serve relevant ads).

<a id="advertising"></a>
## 2. Advertising and Analytics
We use **Google AdMob** to serve advertisements (such as banners, interstitials, and rewarded video ads). 
- **Personalized Ads:** Depending on your region and your consent choices, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show you personalized ads that are more relevant to you.
- **Managing Your Ad Choices:** You can change your consent for personalized ads at any time. Simply open the **Settings** menu in Unbound, look under the "Privacy & Ads" section, and tap **Manage ad choices**. This will reopen the privacy options form.

<a id="purchases"></a>
## 3. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions
We offer a "Premium" in-app purchase that removes advertisements. To process these transactions, we use **RevenueCat** and your platform's native billing system (Google Play Billing or Apple App Store).
- We do not process or store your payment details (like credit card numbers) directly.
- RevenueCat receives anonymous transaction data (such as purchase receipts and entitlement status) to securely unlock premium features across your devices.

<a id="rights"></a>
## 4. Your Privacy Rights
Depending on where you live (such as the EEA, UK, or certain US states), you may have specific rights regarding your personal data:
- **Right to Access/Portability:** You can request to know what data has been collected about you.
- **Right to Deletion:** You can request that your data be deleted.
- **Right to Opt-Out:** You can opt-out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising (manageable via the "Manage ad choices" button in-app or your device settings).
- **Right to Correction:** You can request corrections to inaccurate data.
